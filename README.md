# NextStepAcademy

**Exceptional private tutors for Kindergarten to Year 12, in your home or online, across Australia.**

NextStepAcademy started in Sydney with one belief: the right tutor can change a student's results, their confidence and the way they feel about school. Today we match families right across Australia with hand-picked private tutors, in-home across Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Perth, Adelaide, Canberra and the Gold Coast, and online anywhere in the country.

### What we tutor

- Every subject from Kindergarten to Year 12, including Maths, English and the sciences
- HSC, VCE, QCE, SACE, WACE and the IB
- NAPLAN, OC and selective school test preparation
- One-to-one lessons that follow your child's school syllabus and are built around their goals

### Our tutors

Our tutors are state rankers, duxes and high ATAR achievers, but more importantly they are people who inspire confidence. They are mentors more than tutors, with strong personalities that make learning stick. Every tutor is hand-picked and holds a current Working With Children Check.
